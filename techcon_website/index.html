TechCon 2024, held from September 8–10 in Austin, Texas, was a major event centered on semiconductor research, innovation, and talent development. The conference featured technical presentations, poster sessions, and a CareerConnections recruiting event that linked students with leading companies in the industry. In her keynote address, Deirdre Hanford, CEO of Natcast, emphasized the importance of continuous R&D in advancing semiconductor technology. The event was notably diverse, with over 50% participation from women and underrepresented minorities, as well as international attendees from countries such as Switzerland, India, Germany, Taiwan, Canada, and the United States. Major companies like Intel, TSMC, AMD, Qualcomm, Samsung, and Arm actively recruited students. TechCon concluded by recognizing top performers with awards for the best student and undergraduate presenters.
